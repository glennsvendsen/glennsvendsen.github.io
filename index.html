Hi, I'm Glenn.

I'm a product person based in Oslo. I like taking complicated systems and making them feel simple, and I'm happiest somewhere between the people using a thing and the people building it.

On the side I build small apps and games, usually with AI as a pair programmer, to learn what's possible and keep my hands dirty. Away from a screen I'm running, halfway up a mountain, or polishing a motorcycle I should probably be riding.

Off the clock

Away from the screen.

When I'm not building something, I'm usually outside, running, hiking or riding my Bonnie. The rest of the time I'm with the family, eating fried rice, and later, once the kids are in bed, enjoying fermented grapes.

Fig. 01

Running

Slow enough to think, fast enough to call it training. My better ideas tend to turn up around kilometre five, with nothing to write them down on.

Fig. 02

Mountains

Hiking anywhere with a summit and a view that makes the climb look like it was a good idea all along. Being outdoors in general, really.

Fig. 03

Two wheels

A Triumph Bonneville: classic lines, a parallel twin, and an owner who spends almost as much time looking at it as riding it.

Fig. 04

Fried rice

Dinner with the family, with fried rice whenever I get a say. The one release nobody has ever complained about.

In short: if it's outdoors or has a power button, I'm probably interested.
