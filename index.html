Hi, I'm Glenn.

I'm a product person based in Oslo. I like taking complicated systems and making them feel simple, and I'm happiest somewhere between the people using a thing and the people building it.

I've spent thirteen years in tech, in commercial roles and, for the last five, in product. Before that I was a wedding and press photographer, and I studied journalism and political science. On the side I build small apps and games, usually with AI as a pair programmer, to learn what's possible and keep my hands dirty.

How this page works.

It looks simple. Under the paper, a few things are going on:

  • Drawn on paper, then in code. I sketched Oslo by hand first. A script then draws the skyline stroke by stroke and wobbles it to look like pencil.
  • The real sun and moon. Sunrise and sunset are calculated for Oslo, and the moon shows its true phase, tilt, height, rise and set. The clock on the City Hall tower shows the right time too 🙂
  • Live weather. Rain, snow, fog and cloud fall in the picture, snow piles up on the ridges, and the fjord freezes after a long cold spell.
  • Small surprises. Windows light up after dark, and 17 May, Christmas and New Year's Eve have their own. On clear winter nights I paint an aurora, as decoration, not a forecast.
  • Light on the web. No frameworks, no build step, no cookies. The fonts are served from this site, and visit stats are anonymous.
  • A note in the post. The form is sent by a small Cloudflare Worker straight to my inbox. Cloudflare Turnstile keeps the bots out, and only loads once you start writing.
